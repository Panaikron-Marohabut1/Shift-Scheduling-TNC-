# User Journey

What each role does in the Shift Schedule system (ShiftFlow) as of 17 November 2026. Automated checks are listed in the [Feature List](feature-list.md#automated-features).

## 1. Shift Employee

- Sign in and see today's shift, consecutive working days and remaining swap quota (My Shift)
- View personal and team shift schedules
- Submit a shift swap request with an employee from another team
- Submit a leave request, with or without a cover person
- Submit an OT request for one's own day off
- Submit a public holiday request
- Track request status and approval steps
- Withdraw a pending request, or cancel an approved request before the shift starts
- View personal history
- Read notifications

## 2. Shift Supervisor

- Everything a Shift Employee can do for their own shifts; the Supervisor's own requests are approved by the Manager
- View the team schedule and the daily staffing overview
- Review the request queue
- Approve or reject requests, giving a reason when rejecting
- View the change history
- Read notifications

## 3. Manager

- Monitor the status of every request
- Approve or reject requests submitted by Supervisors
- View the shift schedule and the annual schedule
- Manage people and teams: change position, switch role between Employee and Supervisor, transfer team from an effective date
- Set the monthly swap quota for the next month
- Read notifications

## 4. HR

- View the combined shift schedule of teams A–D
- View the closed-month roster and download it as CSV for Excel
- Review OT records and history
- Read notifications

## 5. Admin

- See users who have signed in but have no role yet
- Assign each user's first role: Employee, Supervisor or Manager
- View accounts and role-assignment history

## 6. External User (Van Driver)

- View the employee transport schedule
- Acknowledge the schedule
- Read notifications

## 7. Pending User

- After signing in without a role, see a waiting page until an Admin assigns one
